Quoted in The Indian Express alongside senior hospital physicians

In a national feature on GLP-1 weight loss therapy, The Indian Express cited Amit Baruna by name — alongside a Head of Diabetology and a Consultant Bariatric Surgeon — as the metabolic expert whose case study illustrated what physicians were missing. His Instagram post on the case was embedded directly in the article.

The case: A 52-year-old woman whose body was in survival mode. Even GLP-1 couldn't help her. She used Mounjaro for eight weeks — zero fat loss. Her metabolism was not slow. It was literally shut down.

The intervention: "We made her stop for two weeks. No intense training. We added 90 minutes of extra sleep. Some walk post-dinner. Targeted supplement. Only then does her metabolism respond. Not to pressure but to support" — Amit Baruna, The Indian Express

The outcome: Metabolism responded. Fat loss resumed. The case became a national reference point for GLP-1 lifestyle integration.

The Beginning

I was that coach. Loud, energetic, convinced that the right programme could fix anything. I genuinely believed hard work was the answer for everyone. That belief didn't last long.

01 / 08
Fitness First · Mar 2017 – Dec 2018

I was working with some of the most committed people I'd ever met. Showing up every day. Following the plan. And some of them just... weren't changing. Same effort. Different results. I couldn't explain it. That bothered me more than I let on.

02 / 08
Cult.fit · Dec 2018 – Feb 2021

Every now and then I'd get a case — someone doing everything right and still failing. It wasn't discipline. It wasn't diet. Something deeper was breaking. I just didn't have the language for it yet.

03 / 08
FitMeNow · Jan 2021 – Dec 2025

I built my own centre and consulted 2,000+ people who nobody else had answers for. The more cases I saw, the clearer it became — for a lot of these people, the problem wasn't what they were eating or how they were training. Something else was off. I just hadn't found the exact words for it yet.

04 / 08
The Search

The answers weren't in any programme. They weren't in any certification. I started reading everything — sleep science, stress physiology, gut health, inflammation, circadian rhythm. Book after book. I wasn't looking for a new protocol. I was looking for the pattern that connected all of it. And slowly — it started to appear.

05 / 08
HealthifyMe · Sep 2021 – Dec 2025

This is where it all started clicking. Deep blood work. CGM data. Physician referrals. GLP-1 cases. 2,000+ consultations. The data kept pointing to the same thing — it wasn't the diet. It wasn't the workout. It was sleep. It was stress. It was circadian rhythm. The body wasn't broken. It just hadn't been given permission.

06 / 08
The Indian Express Moment

This was the moment that broke the common belief. Even the strongest fat loss drug available couldn't move the needle — because the metabolism was completely shut down. I was able to fix something that the most powerful GLP-1 drug couldn't fix alone. That case ended up in The Indian Express alongside senior hospital physicians.
